# Studio 1: designlink

## 360 m² Misaki
"Architecture is supposed to complete nature. Great architecture makes nature more beautiful,  it gives it power" -Claudio Silvestrin-. 
Misaki: (in Japanese) means "A Beautiful Bloom". 
Cozy home, filled with nature and natural daylight, a space with resort vibes.
These were our objectives when we first started the design of this home. 
On this corner plot, we emphasised on space openness and designed the structure with an L shape opening towards the corner, celebrating it and allowing for a grand family garden which will be dedicated for a future expansion. 
Inspired by the delicate unfolding of a flower, this villa reflects a home that opens not with grandeur, but with gentle intention, welcoming light, air and the serenity of nature, every detail used in the design from the main structure, to the smalles design feature, represents this concept done with simple, elegant Japanese modern style inside and outside. 
"MISAKI" Residence
"Al Marzouqi" Villa
Location: Al Suyooh Suburb - Sharjah - UAE 


## 460 m² The Cove
“My house is my refuge, an emotional piece of architecture, not a cold piece of convenience" - Luis Barragan 
In a beautiful angular plot, facing the street on multiple angles but surrounded with neighbours from other angles. 
With the objective of creating a family home that is open and welcoming, yet provide a private and safe retreat for the family. 
The best design approach was to make the form of the building follow the angular shape of the plot to maximise the use of space, engraving a big piece of the structure in the back area to provide a protected family space covered by the projections from the first floor which created a safe haven for the family "a Cove" which is the heart of the house and host most of the family's activities. 
The "COVE" residence
Location: UAE - Sharjah - Al Suyooh Suburb - Tilal city.


## 550 m² Nexus
"Families are like branches on a tree. We grow in different directions,yet our roots remain as one." 
NEXUS: a connection linking two or more things; the central and most important point or place. 
It was all about the "connection" in this home; with the sole intention of creating a family home that enhances and protects the family connections. 
In a beautiful land on a corner, we started by placing the building at the back and oriented to the front to exploit the cooling wind coming from the front and create a grand entrance with a big front family garden. 
We emphasised on the connectivity factor by creating uninterrupted viewpoints and visual lines going inwards and stepping outwards of the space, with open space planning and carefully studdied circulation with a direct connection to the family garden, at the same time, provided protection and privacy from the street with an elegant privacy screen that kept the indoor spaces protected which will help in creating a loving family environment that enhances their connection (NEXUS) and protects their bonds. 
NEXUS Residence
"Al-Maazmi Villa".
Location: Al reeman - Abu Dhabi - UAE

## 600 m² Eliorin
"The essence of architecture is space, light, and order." Le Corbusier. 
Minimal, modern, simple, and elegant family home, were our objectives for the design of this home. 
Eliorin: Inspired by the fusion of Elio (sun) and Serein (rain), the design emphasizes an unobstructed connection between the indoors and the outdoors. 
The villa is carefully laid out to encourage family interaction, with open spaces that promote togetherness while also offering areas for quiet reflection. Expansive open garden spaces invite fresh air and light, allowing the home to breathe and flow seamlessly with nature. 
Enveloped in light structure shade that mimics the soft pattern of raindrops, offering the feeling of being embraced by a gentle shower, with each drop symbolizing comfort and serenity. This unique design captures the essence of rain—bringing a cosy ambience that draws the family together, enhancing moments of warmth and connection. 
"ELIORIN" Residence
"Al Khazraji" villa
Location: Riyad City - Abu Dhabi - UAE
